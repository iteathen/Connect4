# Blanket resource-overlap exclusion

**Canonical claim:** C4-R0031

U1 Test B V2 treated overlapping resources as generically incompatible. Against the cited Allis compatibility oracle it produced 10,938 false negatives among 695,232 comparisons, localized to A5/A6 with A1 around the claim/inverse boundary-sharing seam.

The blanket rule is disproven in that oracle domain. The later interval/boundary-sharing V3 is a candidate replacement, but its 82,367 extra accepted pairs are not yet proven sound.
