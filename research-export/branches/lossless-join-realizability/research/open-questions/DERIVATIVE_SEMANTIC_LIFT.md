## What remains to prove

A future theorem must explain how exact structural coordinates acquire game-semantic authority while preserving at least:

- gravity/support accessibility;
- player/turn ownership;
- shared resource conflicts and reservations;
- blocker clauses without silently replacing OR by XOR;
- completion-before-deadline and first-win stopping;
- realizability/correlation lost by a quotient;

The key question is therefore not whether another linear K=4 derivative is missing. C4-R0057/R0058 rule that out within the one-dimensional linear window hierarchy.
