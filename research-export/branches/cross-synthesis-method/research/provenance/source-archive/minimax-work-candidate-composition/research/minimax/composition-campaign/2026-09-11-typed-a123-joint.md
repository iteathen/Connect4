## Qualification discipline

For each frozen anchor the harness ran three forms eight times in rotating order:

1. typed MQ5 without A123;
2. Map/WSL-625 + geometry A123;
3. typed MQ5 + geometry A123.

The first two repetitions were excluded as warmup. The six timed repetitions contain each cyclic order twice.

Every repetition required:

- exact frozen root score;
- frozen typed-baseline and A123 node counts;
- exact Map+A123 vs typed+A123 equality for nodes, TT hits, TT write attempts/successes, forced transitions and draw stops;
- exact state-arena parity for states, intern hits and move preparations;
- exact side-arena parity for side states, stored requirement IDs, mover/blocker transition-cache entries, normalization calls and intern hits;
- exact A123 check/hit/cut/candidate/DFS-step parity.

The A123 stage remains a one-sided `score <= 0` certificate at non-forced TT-miss decision states. Its cutoff is published only as an upper TT bound. Typed hashing is never identity authority.

## Interpretation

The two mechanisms compose cleanly in **semantics and proof volume**:

- exact scores are preserved;
- the full previously measured A123 proof-node reduction survives typed interning with no node drift;
- state, side-state, move-preparation and transition-cache populations all shrink with the A123 proof tree;
- typed representation remains materially faster than Map/BigInt representation on the exact same A123 tree.

They are **not multiplicatively additive in wall time in this tested implementation form**. Once MQ5 interning/transition machinery is made substantially cheaper, geometry A123's remaining per-decision work becomes large enough to outweigh its proof-volume savings on both frozen anchors. The effect is modestly negative on the loss anchor and clearly negative on the win anchor.

This does **not** reject A123. It scopes a new saturation/cost interaction:

`MQ5-TYPED-OPENADDR-V1 + A123-GEOMETRY-U1U2-V4 + current decision-stage implementation + these exact-distance anchors`

The certificate remains strongly proof-positive. Future A123 forms should target the now-exposed cost center (candidate generation/coverage invocation frequency/cheap preconditions or reuse), rather than questioning the certificate semantics.

Likewise, typed interning remains positive under A123; its speedup falls from the earlier 1.493x/1.571x typed-vs-Map baseline measurements to 1.381x/1.300x on the smaller A123 trees, which is an observed workload/saturation interaction rather than a failure of typed identity.
