# Dead-residual hot-state maintenance

**Related claims:** C4-R0009, C4-R0010

The dead-residual theorem (C4-R0009) remains an exact guarded result. What is rejected/deferred for the current measured hot path is the investigated strategy of continuously maintaining additional live state/counters solely to detect this certificate.

The terminal-frontier benchmark campaign found effectively no useful detection incidence on the measured workload while the deeper-search implementation paid transition/runtime cost. That makes the maintenance mechanism unattractive under current evidence.

This record must not be cited as evidence against the theorem. A future representation that makes dead-residual detection essentially free, or a workload with materially higher incidence, can reopen the implementation question without reopening theorem truth.
