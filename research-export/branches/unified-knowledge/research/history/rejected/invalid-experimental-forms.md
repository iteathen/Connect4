# Invalid or non-authoritative experimental forms

These forms failed before they could provide evidence about their parent mechanism.

- **DEAD first exact implementation:** published exact TT values after alpha-beta cutoffs. Invalid exactness form; do not use it as evidence against DEAD/neutral-tempo abstraction.
- **U1 Test B V1:** descriptor computed compatibility claims but failed to return them. Harness failure; no candidate evidence.
- **First typed MQ5 draft:** omitted required antichain normalization and singleton winning-count metadata. Corrected before timing evidence was accepted; do not use the draft against typed exact interning.
- **First MQ4 flat replay qualifier:** recomputed semantic children instead of replaying encoded transition entries. Qualification weakness was repaired by materialize/serialize/decode/replay testing; do not treat the weak harness as final evidence.

These examples motivate C4-R0035: mechanism, implementation form and harness validity must remain distinct.
