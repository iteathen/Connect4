## 1. Subject / declared scope — QU `^95002`

RBA is the currently unknown **complete algebraic structure sufficient to recover exact ordinary strong value, exact legal action values, and therefore exact best move for standard 7x6 Connect4 from structural game information**.

This QU is initially scoped to:

```text
standard board      7 x 6
connect length      4
ordinary gravity play
alternating no-pass ownership
immediate first-win stopping
exact strong value:
    win > draw > loss
    faster win preferred
    slower loss preferred
```

A Connect-K family generalization is an open structural question, not part of the current realization-family guarantee.

RBA denotes **semantic/algebraic structure**, not one evaluation schedule or implementation. Recursive, iterative, GPU, symbolic, or table-driven execution may evaluate the same algebra without becoming different RBA semantics merely because their control flow differs.

## 3. Open structural region O — QU `^95004`

The unknown is decomposed into explicit open carriers rather than one opaque token.

### O1 — minimal operator basis

Unknown:

> What is the smallest exact generating set of operations/relations from which complete RBA value semantics follow?

Known candidate fragments include residual-shape lattice operations, cofactors, adjoints, threshold boundaries, and blocker duality. Their minimality and completeness as one basis are not established.

### O2 — compactness law

Unknown:

> What structural parameter controls the size of exact RBA boundaries/intermediate duals toward the empty root?

Candidate parameters include:

- residual-shape poset width/height;
- number and interaction of join-irreducibles;
- minimal-cover width;
- closed-blocker width;
- hypergraph/closure-system VC dimension;
- incidence/path/tree-width-like quantities.

No root-scale compactness theorem is currently established.

### O3 — rank-collapse / iteration index

Unknown:

> Is rank-wise propagation essential, or can several/all ranks be composed algebraically by idempotence, bounded index, nilpotence, transfer composition, exponentiation, or another exact law?

Do **not** classify this binary question as semantic QU `UNRESOLVED` yet. Current evidence has not established both realization branches.

### O4 — direct-root expression

Unknown:

> Does an exact finite expression/operator composition map the empty-board structural object directly to the exact action values/best move without explicit rank-by-rank iteration?

This is stronger than existence of an algebraic fixed-point recurrence.

### O5 — minimal observation / further quotient

Unknown:

> Is the support-local residual-shape lattice the minimal sufficient ordinary-value carrier, or does a strictly coarser exact observation exist?

Any proposed collapse must preserve exact value and respect unknown potentially load-bearing distinctions.

### O6 — proof/value correspondence

Unknown:

> What exact relationship, if any, connects RBA ordinary-value structure to the predecessor-closed clause/proof carrier and the missing-law claims C4-R0043, C4-R0069, and C4-R0076?

Apparent correspondence remains claim-level until independently proved. This open role explicitly prevents those claims from being silently identified.

### O7 — family scope

Unknown:

> Which RBA laws generalize from fixed 7x6 to fixed-K variable boards or arbitrary finite gravity Connect-K geometries, and with what complexity bounds?

### O8 — canonicality / factorization

Unknown:

> Are current residual/upset/Upper-Lower/blocker factorizations canonical under a useful comparison profile, merely one lawful factorization, or reducible to a smaller equivalent structure?

## 4. Possibility universe Ω — QU `^95005`

The admissible universe is defined intensionally.

An RBA realization is any algebraic completion/factorization of the current research information state that:

1. has the declared standard-7x6 subject and ordinary-value meaning;
2. preserves every fixed authority-1.1 fact and explicit exclusion;
3. supplies exact state/action strong value on every legal state;
4. respects first-win stopping and legal support/action semantics;
5. preserves all constraints declared below;
6. may use a conservative abstract superdomain, provided restriction to legal states is exact;
7. may use rank iteration or an exact rank-collapsed equivalent;
8. may choose a different lawful factorization when a qualified bridge preserves the same semantics;
9. does not treat implementation schedule, solver brand, or cache layout as an algebraic primitive without proof that it is semantically load-bearing.

The universe does **not** include arbitrary heuristics, approximations, or structures that merely agree on sampled solved positions.

## 5. Constraints C — QU `^95006`

Every admissible realization must satisfy:

### C1 exactness

```text
for every legal state s:
    V_RBA(s) = exact ordinary strong value(s)

for every legal action a:
    Q_RBA(s,a) = exact ordinary strong action value(s,a)
```

### C2 legal transition fidelity

A legal action must have the same landing event and immediate terminal token as the governed Connect4 semantics. Nonterminal structural transition must preserve the exact ordinary future-value meaning.

### C3 first-win stopping

Post-terminal hypothetical distinctions cannot influence ordinary value.

### C4 legal-subset safety

If a realization uses a larger abstract residual/value superdomain, legal states must remain semantically protected: abstract states outside the legal subset may not introduce false value claims on legal states.

### C5 value/proof identity separation

RBA ordinary-value equivalence does not authorize equality of proof objects, certificate provenance, history, CPC/NDC side state, or advisory state not derivable from the RBA observation.

### C6 no oracle-premise substitution

Solved databases, finite W/D/L samples, and benchmark labels may falsify or qualify candidate laws but are not theorem premises establishing RBA.

### C7 relation promotion gate

Suspected relations among post-1.1 claims remain claim metadata until their own deductive or guarded-exact proof burden is met.

### C8 observation-first revision

A mismatch first updates the evidence/constraint picture. It does not permit narrowing Ω merely to preserve a desired algebraic hypothesis.

## 9. Known structural fragments and evidence — QU `^95028`

These constrain discovery but retain their own source statuses.

### K1 — residual superdomain conservative-extension candidate

Legal q/value behavior can be embedded into a larger residual-function domain without treating every abstract residual pair as legal q.

### K2 — coordinate-separable fixed-action predecessor

The mover and opponent residual coordinates admit separate one-cell predecessor treatment before recombination of coordinate frontiers.

### K3 — residual-antichain/upset distributive lattice

Normalized residual antichains correspond to upsets of the support-local residual-shape poset; join/meet correspond to union/intersection of those upsets.

### K4 — cofactor adjoint candidate

The one-cell cofactor is join-preserving on the upset lattice and has exact order-theoretic preimage operations, including a finite right-adjoint form for lower constraints and a minimal-cover frontier for upper constraints.

### K5 — Upper strong-value boundaries

Exact score-threshold regions are represented by minimal generators under the support-local favorable order in the tested/derived value algebra.

### K6 — closed-blocker Lower dual candidate

For adjacent discrete strong-score levels, Lower may be derived as the closure-aware complement/dual of the next Upper boundary.

### K7 — current scaling evidence

The value-boundary path has bypassed the original rank-35 distributed proof wall and has advanced through rank-32, with rank-31 Upper closed and Lower-dual work active at the latest pre-RBA checkpoint.

These are evidence/fragments, not a declaration that the complete RBA has been found.

## 13. Refinement rule — QU `^95020`

Future work should refine this QU only when new qualified information genuinely narrows `R(RBA)` while preserving the same declared subject/meaning.

Examples of legitimate refinement:

- proof that one open operator relation is necessary;
- counterexample excluding an entire candidate factorization;
- a compactness theorem;

Examples requiring semantic revision rather than simple refinement:

- changing the target from ordinary strong value to proof-certificate identity;
- replacing standard 7x6 scope with a different game family without an explicit generalization profile;
- redefining “RBA” to require a particular implementation schedule.
