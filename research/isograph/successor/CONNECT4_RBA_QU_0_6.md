## Algebraic form

For one threshold front, the two-ply Bellman transformer has the schematic form:

~~~text
T(U)
  =
  OR over current-player actions a
    (
      terminal-win constant
      OR
      AND over legal opponent replies b
        Pre_ab(U)
    )
~~~

where `Pre_ab` is the exact composed q/cofactor predicate preimage with terminal semantics.

So the symbolic block operator is a monotone lattice polynomial assembled from:

- exact predicate preimages;
- lattice union;
- lattice intersection;
- terminal constants.

The same structural transformer acts independently on the four endpoint fronts for even-depth blocks. Odd-depth polarity swaps endpoints under negation.

## Stronger homomorphism law rejected

The transformer does not preserve arbitrary joins or meets of input fronts.

Across 40 random pairs on the two pathological supports:

~~~text
join-homomorphism failures    40 / 40
meet-homomorphism failures    29 / 40
~~~

For join:

~~~text
T(A union B)
    strictly contained additional success states
beyond
T(A) union T(B)
~~~

in every trial.

No opposite-direction violation occurred.

## Why: mixed reply covers

Inside one current-player action:

~~~text
AND_b (Pre_b(A) OR Pre_b(B))
~~~

allows different opponent replies to be discharged by different alternatives.

It therefore contains mixed terms absent from:

~~~text
(AND_b Pre_b(A))
OR
(AND_b Pre_b(B))
~~~

This is the load-bearing non-homomorphic operation.

It is exactly the same structural shape as:

- minimal reply covers;
- distributive-lattice antichain products;
- minimal transversals / blockers;
- positive alternating-automata formulas.
