## Existing qualified structure that directly satisfies NEES

The 0.3 graph and subsequent discovery already support several NEES requirements:

- q identity remains semantic content, not worker/pool/cache address identity;
- exact hashes are locators only;
- recursive worker storage is prepared/sealed before entry;
- worker-local IDs remain non-portable;
- apply/undo preserves exact reversible state instead of cloning/replay;
- native frontier facts precede general recursion;
- manager RPC / queue scanning remain outside ordinary recursive nodes;
- scheduling control is amortized rather than per node;
- diagnostics/reporting are outside recursive execution;
- q visibility delay and duplicated work are represented as execution-economic uncertainty rather than semantic ambiguity.

NEES adoption therefore formalizes much of the qualified direction rather than replacing it.

## Draft 0.3 maximal-effort baseline

The first qualification under this 0.2 binding MUST inventory the complete declared E0-E2 IsoMax hot scope and durably disposition reasonably visible machine-cost structure, including:

- repeated calls and dispatch;
- branches and serial dependency chains;
- loads/stores and memory traffic;
- allocation/lifetime/GC;
- boxing/conversion and numeric representation;
- hashing/probing/scanning;
- Atomics/synchronization/coherence;
- copies and transport that occur at E0-E2 cadence;
- runtime/JIT realization where the claim depends on it.

Each known/suspected cost should be classified using the NEES Draft 0.3 disposition vocabulary: REQUIRED, TRADEOFF, UNAVOIDABLE-PROFILE, COSTED-OUT, REMOVED, SUPERSEDED, UNVERIFIED-DEBT, or DEVIATION.

Known exact candidates such as F-002 must not disappear merely because another bottleneck is larger. Larger costs may be prioritized first.

After this baseline exists, later PRs inherit it and only re-audit the affected causal neighborhood and invalidated assumptions.

## Qualification cadence

Use the NEES coherent-PR qualification boundary.

During development:

- reuse inherited qualified graph/runtime evidence;
- checkpoint meaningful findings durably;
- use targeted checks only when needed to choose the next implementation step or protect a prerequisite invariant;
- do not benchmark/profile/test the entire system after each optimized line.

- run exact semantic controls;
- perform NEES conformance review;
- run applicable structural/deterministic detectors;
- inspect V8/runtime realization when the implementation claim depends on it;
- perform paired performance qualification when performance is a promotion premise;
- preserve material negative results and falsifiers.
