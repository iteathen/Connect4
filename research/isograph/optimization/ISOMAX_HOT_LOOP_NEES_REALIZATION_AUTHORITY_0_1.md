## Existing qualified structure that directly satisfies NEES

The 0.3 graph and subsequent discovery already support several NEES requirements:

- q identity remains semantic content, not worker/pool/cache address identity;
- exact hashes are locators only;
- recursive worker storage is prepared/sealed before entry;
- worker-local IDs remain non-portable;
- apply/undo preserves exact reversible state instead of cloning/replay;
- native frontier facts precede general recursion;
- manager RPC / queue scanning remain outside ordinary recursive nodes;
- scheduling control is amortized rather than per node;
- diagnostics/reporting are outside recursive execution;
- q visibility delay and duplicated work are represented as execution-economic uncertainty rather than semantic ambiguity.

NEES adoption therefore formalizes much of the qualified direction rather than replacing it.
