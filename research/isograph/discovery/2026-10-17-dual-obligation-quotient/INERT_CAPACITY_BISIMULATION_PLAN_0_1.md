## The new structural lead
Previous full-future fixed-column transport certifies WDL isomorphisms and scalar P1 branch pruning even beyond reflection, but cannot relate positions with different multisets of remaining column heights. The remaining research objective is to eliminate real branches **without exploring continuations**, retaining every tied-optimal literal move.

For legal nonterminal source q=(h,R0,R1), call a column c **fully inert** if no future cell (c,r), r>=h[c], occurs in ANY generator of R0 or R1. It must have remaining capacity H-h[c]>0. Since owner contraction and opponent blocking only contract/delete source generators, a fully inert column remains inert after any future move. Placing any stone into it cannot complete a winning line or change either residual family. The sole effects are: consume one unit of gravity-legal *neutral capacity* and switch the mover; it may trigger a full-board draw at the same final rank.

Let I(q) be all such legal inert columns, and K(q)=sum_(c in I(q)) (H-h[c]) their total remaining neutral capacity. Replace all columns in I(q) by ONE distinguished **neutral-token action** available iff K>0. Preserve all remaining active columns with their exact original supports, owner residual antichains and move labels. After any active move, recompute I from the contracted residuals; any newly fully-inert columns transfer their remaining capacities to the neutral pool. Then (active support, residual requirements, K, mover/rank) is a quotient state.

**Theorem candidate:** any two legal first-win-stopped states with identical labelled ACTIVE column heights, identical R0/R1 on active future cells, equal summed neutral capacity K, and same mover have isomorphic entire remaining WDL games (including first-win, draw), even if the original partition of K among inert columns is DIFFERENT. At a source with 2+ inert legal columns, P0/P1 action choice of ANY such column yields the exact SAME scalar future WDL, and **all literal inert actions form one tied WDL action class**, without evaluating either continuation. Their future legal move replies can be matched *dynamically*: active c->active c; an inert move in either game maps to ANY available neutral move of the other, decreasing K by one. Mapping depends on the history's neutral-token allocation and is not one fixed column permutation.

**Potential pitfall:** A merely inactive IMMEDIATE landing cell does not make the column inert if a higher cell appears in any residual. An inert column criterion must inspect **the entire remaining suffix** of that column. It must incorporate source opponent-blocked lines, owner first-win stopping and full-board draw; never treat token consumption as a free pass that fails to change turn parity.
