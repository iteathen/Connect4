## Bounded experimental results

The surviving OOO quotient is not generally an H/V/D three-channel interaction.

On 6x3-k4:

- HVD OOO triples: 0
- non-HVD OOO triples: 50
- HVD-only image rank: 0
- non-HVD image rank: 16
- non-HVD scalar contradictions: 0
- non-HVD scalar image dimension: 2

Thus the full scalar correction survives on a carrier with H only.

On 6x3-k3, HVD-only residue is scalar-exact, but on 4x5-k4 it is not. HVD is therefore not a common sufficient carrier.

The frozen orientation-summary ladder has no common compressed passing rung. ORI_PRESENCE and PAIR_ORI_INCIDENCE fail all three. Count/owner/phase/depth orientation summaries may pass 6x3-k3 but fail 4x5-k4 and/or 6x3-k4.

Common exactness appears only at EXACT_ORIENTATION_PROVENANCE and the full reconstruction control. At that point the orientation feature catalogs have the same cardinality as the used OOO triple catalogs on all three carriers and reproduce the full OOO image ranks 75, 294, and 16.

The same-geometry cross-k comparison 6x3-k3 versus 6x3-k4 also finds no shared scalar-exact compressed orientation feature vocabulary. The only shared orientation-derived feature key occurs in PAIR_ORI_INCIDENCE, which is scalar-inexact on both carriers.

## DTS interpretation

H, V, and D do have different exact support-transition anatomy:

- H is transverse to vertical support chains;
- V lies within one support chain;
- D couples horizontal displacement to changing support depth.

Those distinctions are structurally real, but they are not the common source of the OOO correction.

Any common transition law must admit both mixed-orientation carriers and the horizontal-only 6x3-k4 carrier. The current stronger target is therefore an exchange/support circuit law, not an H/V/D circuit.
