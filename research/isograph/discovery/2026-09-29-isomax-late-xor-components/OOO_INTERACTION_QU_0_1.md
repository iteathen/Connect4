## 6. Exact third-difference invariant

Consider three descriptor oddness coordinates \(O_a,O_b,O_c\).

Hold fixed:

- every descriptor presence bit;
- every other descriptor oddness state;
- every other repaired structural coordinate.

If all eight E/O assignments of \(a,b,c\) exist, define the third finite difference

\[
\Delta_{abc}Y
=
\bigoplus_{\epsilon\in GF(2)^3}

\]

Every function of total degree \(\le2\) in the varied oddness bits cancels.

Because presence is fixed across the cube, a presence-containing cubic term such as

\[

has degree at most 2 in the three varied oddness coordinates and also cancels.

Therefore a nonzero 8-corner result isolates genuine OOO third-order scalar dependence.

If the scalar function is degree \(\le3\) in oddness on the fixed-presence fiber, then

\[
\Delta_{abc}Y

is independent of the fixed context and equals the OOO coefficient vector for that triple.

This is the target of EW-RS-063.

## 7. Outcome-code gauge

Let an injective W/D/L encoding into \(GF(2)^2\) be changed by an affine automorphism

\[
Y\mapsto AY+b,
\qquad
A\in GL(2,2).
\]

Across eight cube corners, the constant \(b\) appears eight times and cancels.

Thus

\[
\Delta^3(AY+b)=A\,\Delta^3Y.
\]

Since \(A\) is invertible,

\[
\Delta^3Y=0
\iff
\Delta^3(AY+b)=0.
\]

Therefore **zero versus nonzero OOO cube interaction is invariant under affine recoding of the W/D/L labels**.

Any injective encoding of three labels into the four points of \(GF(2)^2\) is affinely equivalent to any other such encoding, so nonzero third-difference status is the meaningful code-gauge-invariant certificate.

The specific two-bit vector remains gauge-dependent.

# 15. Exact component-width conservation — new IA

The OOO oddness coordinates are not freely independent.

Every RFG residual-incidence component partitions a subset of the physical columns, and the components partition all W columns. If descriptor d has component width w_d and multiplicity n_d, then exactly

\[
\boxed{
\sum_d w_d n_d=W.
}
\]

This is independent of W/D/L outcomes.

Reducing modulo 2 gives

\[
\boxed{
\bigoplus_{d:\,w_d\text{ odd}} O_d
=
W\bmod2.
}
\]

Call this **IA-OOO-09 — width-parity conservation**.

Consequences:

1. the oddness vector lies on an affine parity fiber rather than the full Boolean cube;
2. toggling one odd-width descriptor oddness bit independently is impossible while every other oddness bit is fixed;
3. a complete axis-aligned E/O 3-cube can exist only if every independently toggled coordinate is compatible with all exact width constraints; the mod-2 width law alone excludes any cube containing an independently toggled odd-width descriptor;
4. because the deterministic OOO quotient bases are dominated by width-1 descriptors, absence of direct 3-cubes is structurally expected rather than evidence against OOO dependence.

## 15.1 Exact cubic gauge relations generated by width parity

Let

\[
U=\{d:w_d\text{ odd}\},
\qquad
c=W\bmod2.
\]

The exact structural constraint is

\[
c\oplus\bigoplus_{d\in U}O_d=0.
\]

Multiply by any pair \(O_aO_b\), \(a\ne b\):

\[
0
=
cO_aO_b
\oplus
\bigoplus_{d\in U}O_aO_bO_d.
\]

Terms with \(d=a\) or \(d=b\) reduce by Boolean idempotence to degree two.

Therefore, modulo the complete degree-\(\le2\) span,

\[
\boxed{
\bigoplus_{d\in U\setminus\{a,b\}}
[O_aO_bO_d]
=
0.
}
\]

This is an outcome-independent family of exact relations among OOO cubic columns.

It explains part of the cubic-basis gauge freedom structurally: the deterministic basis is choosing representatives in a quotient already constrained by board-width conservation.

## 15.2 Full integer ZOE width budget

The integer equation gives more than parity.

For one descriptor state define the minimum count

\[
m(Z)=0,\qquad m(O)=1,\qquad m(E)=2.
\]

Every exact multiplicity compatible with ZOE has form

\[
n_d=m(z_d)+2k_d,\qquad k_d\ge0.
\]

Hence every repaired signature satisfies

\[
\boxed{
W
=
B(S)
+
2\sum_d w_dk_d,
}
\]

where

\[
B(S)=\sum_d w_dm(z_d).
\]

Necessary realizability conditions include:

\[
B(S)\le W,
\qquad
W-B(S)\equiv0\pmod2.
\]

For small board widths this sharply restricts which E/O combinations can coexist and can force exact multiplicities from ZOE alone.

This provides a structural explanation for why ZOE was partition-equivalent to full multiplicity on several bounded carriers and nearly equivalent on others.

## 15.3 Revised OOO interpretation

The OOO problem is therefore not best represented as an arbitrary 3-uniform hypergraph on independent Boolean vertices.

A more faithful object is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{odd descriptor support constrained to a weighted component-width fiber}
}
\]

with scalar output defined only on the reachable subset of that fiber.

The cubic polynomial is one coordinate realization of a function on this constrained set.

This makes a new realization family live:

### QU-OOO-05 — weighted-partition / constrained-manifold law

Possibility:

the apparent OOO interactions are induced, wholly or partly, by a lower-complexity scalar law on the weighted ZOE partition of the fixed board width, and cubic terms arise when that law is embedded into unconstrained GF(2) coordinates.

Required tests:

- affine dimension and parity-check structure of each fixed-presence oddness fiber;
- whether all observed cubic quotient dependencies are generated by width conservation plus additional explicit structural constraints;
- whether the scalar residual simplifies after quotienting the oddness coordinate ring by those constraints.

No such stronger conclusion is admitted yet.
