## Why this checkpoint matters

The first assertion-collapse campaign established that residual cofactors:

- are generated by principal residual shapes;
- compose exactly along fixed action words;
- preserve joins;
- do not preserve meets;
- reproduce exact ordinary value on complete bounded controls.

This checkpoint asks what algebraic relation that actually implies and where the large semantic collapse occurs.
