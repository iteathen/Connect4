## Why this checkpoint exists

The semantic RBA core had narrowed to exact antichain-semiring multiplication:

```text
A ⊗ B = Min({a join b})
```

or, in the transformed Lower representation used by the recurrence:

```text
Max({a meet b})
```

The prior high-level implementation made products such as 530,700 and 53,284,344 candidates look like scaling walls.

This checkpoint tests whether that wall is algebraic or representational.

## Exact local-skyline factorization

For one outer generator `a`, define its local projection skyline:

```text
P_a = Max({a meet b | b in B})
```

Then:

```text
Max({a meet b | a in A, b in B})
    =
Max(union over a of P_a)
```

because an element dominated inside one fixed-`a` projection can never become globally maximal.

This gives an exact streaming multiplication algorithm:

1. scan the inner family for one outer generator;
2. absorb dominated products immediately into a small local skyline;
3. append only that local skyline;
4. globally absorb the union of local skylines.

No full Cartesian product need be stored.

## Structural conclusion


This is still ordinary RBA semiring multiplication, but its natural sparse evaluation order matters enormously.
