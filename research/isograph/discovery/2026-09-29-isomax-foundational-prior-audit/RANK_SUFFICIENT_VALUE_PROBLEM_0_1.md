# Rank-sufficient Connect Four value problem

The governing outer target is

[
V_G(P)=D_G(Q_G(P)).
]

Find a **minimal rank-sufficient** structural representation (Q_G(P)) and an exact evaluator (D_G), without assuming either map is recursive, algebraic, polynomial, iterative, closed-form, or any other particular computational shape.

## Rank-local information

At rank (r), (Q_G(P_r)) may use persistent game structure (G), the complete current position, rank/turn, exact facts derivable at that rank, and relevant compressed information carried from earlier ranks. Its definition may not require hypothetical future ranks or ordinary future-tree exploration.

Persistent geometry is rank-local because it is available at every rank.

## Rank-sufficient information

Always name the preserved semantics.

- **Q-V:** scalar game value.
- **Q-A:** exact legal decision/action-value interface under the declared action transporter.
- **Q-F:** complete ordinary future behavior.

Their minimal quotients need not be the same.

## Exact baseline

Qualified Connect4 authority 1.2 already supplies a nonminimal exact baseline on standard 7x6:

[
Q_G(P)=q_o(P),
]

where (q_o) is oriented support plus both owned normalized residual antichains.

Equality of (q_o) preserves the complete literal-action-labelled ordinary future game. Its one-step transition is exact and rank-local. Rank-well-founded Bellman evaluation therefore gives one exact realization of (D_G).

So existence of an exact (Q,D) pair is already established. What remains open is the **minimal/simplest exact pair**: smaller (Q), more direct (D), or both.

The qualified reflection quotient (q_r) is already one exact transporter-aware compression below oriented (q_o).

## Foundational-audit consequence

The bottom-up action-unlabelled/action-token classes used in the cocycle experiments are defined from future child classes. They are therefore not admissible final definitions of rank-local (Q_G) under this objective.

They remain useful **discovery oracles** for which distinctions future behavior eventually forgets. A valid final (Q_G) must recognize the relevant equivalence from current-rank structure, not by ordinary future-tree comparison.

The audited cocycle is diagnostic evidence about representation design, not the outer objective.

## Parallel discovery channels

### Direct/algebraic

Search for exact invariants, finite algebras, factorizations, semiring/polynomial forms, module/linear structure, matrix/tensor operations, canonical normal forms, compact structural coordinate sets, and direct geometry-to-value identities.

A non-iterative exact solution remains fully admissible.

### Rank-local recursive

Search for

[
Q_{r+1}=F_G(Q_r,m_r,G)
]

using safe structural deletion, exact rewrite rules, transporter-aware canonicalization, finite-state closure, compositional interfaces, obligation/certificate maintenance, and proofs that forgotten history is continuation-irrelevant.

A recursively maintained (Q) may still admit a direct (D). A direct (Q) may contain recursively maintained components.

## Minimality

For two representations under the same domain and target semantics, write (Q_1preceq Q_2) when there is an exact rank-local map (h) with

[
Q_1(P)=h(Q_2(P),G).
]

Do not assume a unique global minimum.
