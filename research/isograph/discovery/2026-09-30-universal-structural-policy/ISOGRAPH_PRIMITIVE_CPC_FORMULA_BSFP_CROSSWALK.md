## 3. Difference / derivative geometry

Over \(\mathbb F_2\), a Connect-4 line is a third finite difference:

\[
1+T+T^2+T^3=(1+T)^3=\partial^3.
\]

Response pairs are first derivatives:

\[
1+T=\partial.
\]

Diagonal four-lines are derived mixed-axis derivative relations rather than independent primitive geometry.

This gives a primitive factorization:

- \(\partial^3\): winning requirements;
- \(\partial\): mate/response resources;
- mixed derivatives: cross-axis coupling;
- support order: causal availability;
- CPC event parity: owner/control;
