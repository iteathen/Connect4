## Exact lesson

A well-founded progress rank for positive CPC closure must preserve **proof polarity**, not merely reduce residual cardinality.

The following are individually insufficient as positive ranks:

- pair residual \(\to\) singleton residual;
- smaller residual size;
- more playable attacker singleton targets;
- fewer cells to one geometric winning requirement.

A transition may improve those quantities while simultaneously giving the defender an exact terminal or non-loss certificate elsewhere.

## Required positive-progress guard

A future CPC progress theorem must have the form

\[
\text{structural progress}
+
\text{first-win guard}
+
\text{no opposing CPC closure released by the transition}
\to
\text{stronger attacker proof class}.
\]

The third conjunct is load-bearing.

Equivalently, positive progress belongs in a **typed proof-class automaton**, not in an unsigned residual-size order.

This negative result is consistent with the successful CPC semantic renewal operator:

The semantic-transition principle survives; the unsigned shortcut does not.

Reject:

- pair-star occurrence as a standalone winning certificate;
- pair-to-singleton residual cardinality as a positive value-progress rank;
- setup-6 pair-star as the finite upper-bound solution at the consumed v4 boundary.
