## Legacy target-distance route

At the exact even-rank child:

1. use the frozen surviving Player-1 singleton target from the source state;
2. verify it is still an active P0 singleton in the older quotient kernel;
3. record its exact support distance;
4. require distance in the qualified target-distance engine range 1..2;
5. invoke unchanged `createTargetDistanceLexicographicProofEngine(...).prove(child,target)` with:
   - `maxTargetDistance: 2`;
   - `maxProofStates: 100000`;
   - no root-action restriction;
6. preserve:
   - proof kind;
   - witness / witness kind;
   - lexicographic measure;
   - obligations;
   - rejected actions;
   - proof-engine statistics;
   - resource failure separately from logical rejection.

A child is classified `LEGACY_TARGET_DISTANCE_CLOSED` iff the engine returns `proved: true`.

## Monotonicity interpretation

A positive closure proves a routing integration omission for that exact child: the mathematical theorem already existed, but the newer RCIC router did not consult it.

A logical rejection means this older theorem family does not cover the exact child.

A resource failure is not theorem rejection.
