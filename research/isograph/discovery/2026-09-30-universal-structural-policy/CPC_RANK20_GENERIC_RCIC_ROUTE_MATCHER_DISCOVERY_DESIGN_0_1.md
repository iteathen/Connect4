## Boundedness

The probe stops after:

- one Player-1 candidate move;
- at most one production-CPC-forced Player-2 move;
- at most one deterministic pair-endpoint consumption;
- target-reservoir synthesis/validation.

It performs no recursive search beyond the finite RCIC validation inherent in the existing theorem.
