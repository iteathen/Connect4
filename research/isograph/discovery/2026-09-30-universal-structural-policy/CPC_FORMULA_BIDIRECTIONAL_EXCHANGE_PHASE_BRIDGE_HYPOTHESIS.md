## Bidirectional interpretation if the pilot succeeds

### Formula -> CPC

The formula framework would provide a compact candidate coordinate for identifying whether a local three-channel state is in an obligation-exposure or phase-transfer regime.

This could become an **independent CPC certificate route** after generalization and fresh qualification, potentially avoiding explicit finite-state expansion in some endgames.

### CPC -> formula

CPC would give a mechanistic interpretation to the formula primitives:

- capacity imbalance = imbalance in remaining legal event reservoirs;
- positive-depth imbalance = imbalance in not-yet-released residual obligations;
- boundary bit = live contact with the legal gravity frontier;
- signed exchange = whether capacity change and obligation depth change move together or against one another;
- triangle = three mutually interacting local channels/components.

This is stronger than the rejected H/V/D interpretation because it also exists on horizontal-only carriers.

## Relation to BSFP

The same bridge can later be read backward using BSFP-style controllable predecessors:

## Falsifiers

Reject or narrow the bridge if:

- formula-inspired state coordinates depend on residual IDs rather than exact attachment;
- equal exact RBA states produce different bridge coordinates;
- EXPOSE and TRANSFER transitions collide under every tested equation tuple;
- apparent separation requires adding outcome labels or move-specific exceptions;
- the mapping fails solely because owner information was erased, in which case owner-separated depth counts must be tested before any value interpretation;
- the result depends on support equality while exact RBA orientations differ.
