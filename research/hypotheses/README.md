This directory is for coherent ideas worth testing that are not established enough to enter solver contracts as truth.

Current seeded families:

- `C4-R0013`: whether perfect-play win-set/support/DAG structure exposes initial-board invariants or closed forms that reduce or eliminate recursive derivation of key win-space quantities.
- composition candidates emerging from the predicate ledger, provided their guards and target semantics are explicit.
- [`BSFP_ISOMETRIC_INVARIANT_TRANSFER.md`](BSFP_ISOMETRIC_INVARIANT_TRANSFER.md): cross-lineage candidates for transferring Isometric proof invariants into BSFP without weakening exactness, including reflection orbits, legal-cardinality slice pruning, affine/clause realizability for line-hit states, constraint-aware cone filtering, interval predecessors, typed resource cuts, claim-relative transform reuse, and runtime-earned exact certificates. External solved knowledge remains qualification/falsification only.

- [`BIDIRECTIONAL_REACHABLE_QUOTIENT_BSFP.md`](BIDIRECTIONAL_REACHABLE_QUOTIENT_BSFP.md): runtime-only forward reachable semantic quotient followed by backward BSFP/interval propagation. Existing standard-7x6 forward-growth evidence makes a full graph-first implementation unattractive by itself; the remaining hypothesis is bounded/certificate-producing forward growth combined with structural contraction during construction.
- [`BSFP_DEVICE_CANONICALIZATION_PIPELINE.md`](BSFP_DEVICE_CANONICALIZATION_PIPELINE.md): permutation-first device grouping and representative-only payload interning for O3 output grouping/dense IDs, with exact Connect4 equality retained above generic CUDA scan/sort/compaction.
- **Binary derivative selector/evaluator seam:** a candidate integer/bitwise lexicographic key constructed from exact, certified and advisory structural predicates. Exact closure/terminal fields must remain semantically separate from advisory ordering/evaluation fields. Solved labels, history/killer heuristics and conventional center preference are not authority for the hypothesis. An ordering speedup does not promote a field to correctness authority. Promotion requires a named semantic target, exact guard equivalence and independent proof/qualification. A counterexample consists of either an allegedly exact bit asserting a consequence from only advisory premises, or two states collapsed by a correctness-critical key that require different consequences for the claimed target semantics.

A hypothesis should state what observation would raise confidence and what counterexample would lower or destroy it. If it cannot be falsified or distinguished from adjacent claims, it is not ready for the registry.
