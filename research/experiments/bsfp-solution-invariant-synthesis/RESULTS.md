# Solution-to-solution invariant synthesis: quotient / compute / normalize

Research attribution: **Josh Oshiro**

Date: 2026-09-15

Status: **qualified candidate law; bounded continuation required before broad promotion**

This experiment applies the solution↔solution synthesis method to three independently successful mechanisms:

1. core-relative antichain-product absorption;
2. exact duplicate collapse plus subset-antichain normalization (CUDA-Algorithms #11 boundary);
3. O3 exact semantic occurrence factorization.

The purpose is not to rename three optimizations. The candidate is retained only if the stripped mapping predicts exact work elimination not already supplied by core-relative absorption.

## Target claims

No canonical registry claim is mutated by this note. This is research evidence for a possible common quotient calculus spanning exact semantic identity and order-theoretic dominance.

## Deconstruction

### A. Core-relative product absorption

**Objects acted upon**

Two finite antichains `A` and `B` in a finite set lattice, with universal product generated by join/OR.

**Preserved relation**

Exact element identity and subset order.

**Information discarded**

Compulsory meet/core information is factored out, then whole Cartesian row/column fibers are discarded when a real product witness dominates every result in that fiber.

**Operation it commutes with**

Join/OR product followed by subset-minimal antichain normalization.

**Exactness guard**

For a row `a`, an actual `b in B` must satisfy the core-relative witness law. The witness must correspond to a real product result; a merely hypothetical lower bound is insufficient. The column law is symmetric. Common core is reattached after residual generation.

**Eliminated work**

Product pairs need not be materialized for absorbed rows/columns.

### B. Exact duplicate collapse + subset normalization

**Objects acted upon**

A segmented multiset/sequence of generated fixed- or variable-word lattice elements.

**Preserved relation**

First exact equality, then subset order.

**Information discarded**

Occurrence multiplicity of equal elements, then elements dominated under the requested subset-minimal or subset-maximal direction.

**Operation it commutes with**

Any downstream operation that is extensional in the exact element identity; final antichain semantics are multiplicity-idempotent.

**Exactness guard**

Exact full record equality and exact subset relation inside the same semantic segment. Hashes may index candidates but are not equality authority. Segment boundaries may not be crossed unless an explicit transporter proves identity.

**Eliminated work**

Repeated downstream evaluation of equal results and redundant equality/subset comparisons during normalization.

### C. O3 semantic occurrence factorization

**Objects acted upon**

Concrete transition occurrences whose expensive transform inputs repeat under an authoritative exact semantic identity.

**Preserved relation**

Full canonical semantic equality of the transform input pair.

**Information discarded**

Repeated execution of the same deterministic transform. Concrete occurrences themselves are not discarded.

**Operation it commutes with**

The deterministic semantic transform. One canonical result is computed per exact semantic class and transported/lifted back to all concrete occurrences.

**Exactness guard**

Full canonical equality, plus an explicit occurrence sidecar/transporter. Output slots may not be inferred from unrelated canonical identifiers.

**Eliminated work**

Repeated expensive transform execution while preserving all required occurrence outputs.

## Candidate common law

The first attempted parent law was deliberately stronger than the observation that all three mechanisms "canonicalize" something.

Let `O` be concrete occurrences, `q: O -> Q` an authoritative exact quotient identity, and `f` a deterministic downstream operation. If

`q(x) = q(y) => f(x) = f(y)`

under fixed segment/context metadata, then `f` may be evaluated once per exact `Q` class. What must be restored depends on the target semantics:

- occurrence-sensitive output: retain a transporter/sidecar and lift the result to each occurrence (O3);
- idempotent set/antichain output: multiplicity may be discarded (#11 duplicate collapse);
- ordered idempotent output with a constructive dominance witness: a whole pre-result fiber may be collapsed before generation (core-relative absorption).

This gives a **quotient -> compute/evaluate -> lift-or-normalize** family rather than asserting that the three implementations are the same kernel.

Core-relative absorption is therefore not reduced to equality deduplication. It is a stronger order-witness specialization that can eliminate work before equal results even exist.

## New prediction

If this parent law is useful rather than cosmetic, core-relative absorption should leave some repeated **exact result identities** that can be collapsed before another extensional operation.

For support-local clause coverage, the next operation after product generation is the exact capacity/legal-slice guard. Within one support segment the guard is a deterministic function of:

- the full coverage signature;
- fixed dictionary/coverage metadata for that support;
- the fixed exact stone count.

Therefore exact-equal generated coverage signatures must have the same guard outcome. The candidate predicts that exact result identity may be quotiented **after core-relative absorption but before the capacity guard**, evaluating the guard once per exact result class.

This is new work elimination beyond core-relative absorption itself.

The move is explicitly invalid across unrelated support dictionaries or guard metadata without a proved transporter. A hash is not sufficient identity authority.

## Disposition

**Supports / qualifies the candidate parent law.**

A useful common law exists at least at the level of exact semantic quotienting:

> When a downstream operation is extensional in an authoritative exact identity under fixed context, concrete occurrences may be factored through that identity; restore occurrence information only if the target semantics require it.

The solution-to-solution synthesis produced something new: **exact generated-result grouping may move left of the support-local capacity guard after core-relative absorption**, eliminating repeated guard evaluation without changing the final antichain.

The stronger synthesis claim — that core-relative absorption, normalization, and O3 should become one implementation kernel — is **not supported**. They share a quotient calculus but require different lifting/ordering machinery:

- O3 requires occurrence sidecars;
- duplicate collapse can erase multiplicity;
- core absorption requires constructive subset-dominance witnesses and can act before materialized result identity.

The appropriate supersession relationship is therefore:

`exact semantic quotient factoring`

specialized by

- `occurrence-sensitive quotient + transporter` (O3),
- `idempotent equality quotient + order normalization` (#11 boundary),
- `constructive order-fiber quotient before generation` (core-relative absorption).

This is a theorem-family / shared identity-contract candidate, not yet a single shared CUDA kernel.
