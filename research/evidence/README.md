# Evidence

This directory will hold normalized evidence records attached to stable research claim IDs.

Raw historical benchmark outputs and evidence packets are currently preserved losslessly under `research/provenance/source-archive/**/evidence` and related experiment trees. They should be moved into normalized evidence only after their target claim, workload, oracle, and independence are understood.

Every normalized evidence record should identify:

- claim ID(s) affected;
- evidence kind: proof, exhaustive oracle, independent solver oracle, benchmark, counterexample, property test, replay, measurement, or literature/derivation source;
- exact conditions/workload;
- whether evidence is independent of prior evidence;
- direction: supports, contradicts, constrains, or qualifies;
- strength/update model for empirical claims;
- artifact/source hash or immutable provenance path;
- date and reproduction instructions when applicable.

Repeated measurements generated by the same underlying oracle are not automatically independent Bayesian evidence.
