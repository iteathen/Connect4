# Solver consumption map

Solver families consume the shared claim graph. They do not fork its truth status. `canonical/CLAIM_INDEX.json` is the machine-readable claim root.

## Isometric

Primary role: direct consumer and extension point for the relational structural calculus.

Core inputs:
- structural/win-space model and exact geometry: C4-R0001, R0002, R0057..R0059;
- terminal and forced-move calculus: C4-R0003..R0007, R0009;
- timing, support and residual basis: C4-R0014..R0017, R0025;
- composition target and missing law: C4-R0011, R0018;
- quotient preservation and residual automata: C4-R0008, R0023..R0026;
- strategic certificate evidence: C4-R0029..R0034, R0036..R0038;
- line-hit product structural evidence and its missing realizability law: C4-R0042, R0043;
- residual-pair occurrence reuse as evidence for structural factorization: C4-R0044;
- exact subset-family terminal-subtraction algebra: C4-R0046;
- support-local clause-coverage/cofactor closure evidence: C4-R0073;
- derivative semantic-lift candidate and perfect-play discovery program: C4-R0012, R0013;
- optimal structural-to-terminal selection gap: C4-R0052.

R0057..R0059 are exact geometric/representation inputs, not direct W/D/L authority. R0012 is the separate hypothesis that derivative structure can be lifted into useful game-semantic predicates. R0037 is a negative constraint: Isometric must not inherit the falsified legacy/even-ply A9 responder orientation as though it were a general certificate. R0043 is a positive design constraint: line-hit state cannot silently erase support-local realizability.

## Negamax / Minimax lineage

Primary role: recursive exact solver/control and residual consumer.

It consumes:
- terminal and forced-move certificates: C4-R0003..R0007, R0009;
- quotient/semantic identity requirements: C4-R0008, R0023..R0028;
- certificate and composition work: C4-R0029..R0034;
- joint typed-MQ5+A123 qualification: C4-R0036;
- scoped A9 falsifier and A4/A8/A6 ablation: C4-R0037, R0038;
- exact derivative geometry R0057..R0059 for representation, identity, feature construction or ordering/evaluation research, but not correctness-critical terminal value without an additional guarded theorem;
- line-hit product result as structural/quotient evidence when mapped to the same target semantics: C4-R0042;
- research-method contract for optimization evidence: C4-R0035.

Its exact-search results are especially useful as oracles for candidate structural claims, but an optimization win or loss is not itself a theorem. The binary derivative selector/evaluator remains a hypothesis; ordering performance cannot promote advisory fields into exact value fields.

MQ5/A123 runtime measurements are primarily recursive-solver evidence; BSFP should consume their shared semantic conclusions only when the relevant representation/guard equivalence is explicit.

## Consumption contract

A solver mapping should record:
- research claim ID;
- exact code/contract location consuming it;
- whether consumption is correctness-critical, terminalization, reduction, ordering-only, evaluation-only, or qualification-only;
- equivalence between implementation guards and research guards;
- qualification tests/evidence.

If an implementation uses a weaker/different guard, create a separate implementation claim rather than pretending it implements the canonical theorem unchanged.
