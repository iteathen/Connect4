# Research provenance archive

This directory is the lossless source layer for the Connect4 research consolidation initiated 2026-09-15 under the research direction of Josh Oshiro.

The subtrees under `source-archive/` are exact Git tree objects copied from maximal live research lineages before semantic cleanup. They are evidence and provenance, not current authority. A file appearing here says only that it existed in a source lineage; it does not establish that its claims are accepted.

Canonical research belongs under the top-level `research/` taxonomy. Solver families consume accepted research; they do not own research truth.

Historical research branch heads are preserved by immutable archive tags and committed provenance records before live refs are retired. Live side research branches are not continuity owners. `SOURCE_MANIFEST.json` records exact source identities and refs found to be subsumed by preserved maximal lineages.
