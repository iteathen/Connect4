## Shared interpretation

**CPC** is the timing/ownership relation layer. **WSL-625** is a residual requirement basis. **U1/U2** are primitive ownership/response/blocker relation families. **NDC** is the hypothesis that these relations can be recursively composed into long-horizon proof obligations. **Semantic quotient** asks which physical distinctions can be removed without changing target behavior.

The line-hit product work sharpens that quotient question. The tested BSFP symbolic Win/Loss regions are monotone and often substantially smaller in `(support,H0,H1)` product order (R0042), but that does not license arbitrary line-hit pairs: the support-local realizability image still needs an exact closure law for a direct recurrence (R0043). That is directly relevant to Isometric because it is another instance of the central question: which structural relations are sufficient state, and which hidden compatibility constraints must be retained?

Named strategic rules are treated as certificate families and evidence about this common calculus, not as a separate ontology. A4/A8 provide useful exact cover in the tested regime, while one legacy/even-ply A9 responder orientation is specifically falsified. The negative result is attached to that orientation, not to the whole strategic-rule family.

Isometric is the solver family most directly intended to consume and extend the forward relational calculus. Negamax/Minimax is a recursive residual consumer and exact qualification environment. BSFP consumes much of the same structure backward through symbolic fixed-point propagation. BSFP-specific execution results such as rolling-rank storage (R0040), antichain performance (R0039), the rejected streaming reducer (R0041), and the measured compute wall (R0045) remain solver-scoped unless they establish a solver-neutral structural proposition.

## Current central gap

The research has many exact local facts and several exact/strongly qualified representations. The missing piece remains C4-R0011: a sufficiently complete, guarded composition calculus that turns those relations into global game-theoretic consequences without smuggling recursive enumeration back in. R0043 is now a concrete subproblem of that gap: preserving support-local realizability while operating directly over structural line-hit boundaries.
