## Three different survival concepts that MUST NOT be merged

For each of 69 original four-cell windows L on an ACTUAL colored position s, define:
- E(L): all four original physical cells unoccupied;
- P0only(L): L contains ≥1 P0 stone and zero P1 stones;
- P1only(L): L contains ≥1 P1 stone and zero P0 stones;
- DeadBoth(L): L contains BOTH P0 and P1 stones, so NEITHER player can complete L, EVER, regardless of future play.

These four are an exhaustive disjoint partition of the 69 original windows; all are DIRECT geometrical current-board facts, no strategy search. Set:
- \`U = E+P0only+P1only = 69-DeadBoth\`: DISTINCT original lines still geometrically available to AT LEAST ONE player, counted ONCE.
- \`L0 = E+P0only\`, \`L1=E+P1only\`: owner-specific live lines (count a fully empty window for BOTH players).
- \`S=L0+L1=U+E\`: owner-layer multiplicity (NOT a count of distinct lines).

- \`C0=69-L0\`, \`C1=69-L1\`: opponent-damaged lines (blocked for one named owner) including double-counting D.

**Separate STRATEGIC survivor notion:** A line may be geometrically P0-live but impossible for P0 to complete against an optimal, adapting P1; a line may be physically completable in one cooperative sequence yet not forceable.
